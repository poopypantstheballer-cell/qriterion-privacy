Common questions

Why am I seeing fewer stories than other apps show?
That is the app working. Most items tagged to a company are not news about it — somebody's opinion, a list of stocks, or a story about a different company that a data provider tagged with yours. Those are filtered out. Nothing is deleted: open a company and tap “and N more” to see everything it was tagged with.
Why am I not getting notifications?
Check three things. iOS Settings → Notifications → Qriterion must allow them. In the app, Notifications shows a setting per company — “Announcements only” is the quietest and some companies go weeks without filing anything. And quiet hours, if switched on, hold notifications until the window ends; nothing is dropped while they are on.
Why is a company's price change different from what another app shows?
The figure states the window it covers, next to the number. A change over two years and a change today are different numbers for the same price.
Something says “Not reported”.
The data provider did not supply that figure. The app says so rather than showing a zero, because a zero is a number and a missing figure is not.
Does the app tell me what to buy?
No. It does not score, rank or grade companies, makes no claim about future prices, and does not ask about your finances or goals. It shows what has been published about companies you chose to follow.
How do I delete my account and my data?
Email the address above from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account and everything attached to it. See the privacy policy for what is held and for how long.

Reporting a problem

Email us with the company or screen it happened on, and what you expected to see instead. A screenshot helps. If it is about a specific story, the headline is enough to find it.
